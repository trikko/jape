    Struct RowStream

    A lazy InputRange. Copyable (map/filter need that), but every copy shares one cursor — advancing any of them advances all — and the draining happens exactly once, at the last reference: breaking out of a foreach early still leaves the connection clean.

    struct RowStream(T);

    Every element owns what it points at — a Row keeps its chunk of the result alive — so .array and friends are safe with T = Row as well. Keeping every row of a big stream does keep all of it in memory, though, which is the one thing streaming is meant to avoid.
